M-CHAT-R™
Autism Screening

Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers, Revised. A validated screening tool used worldwide to identify toddlers who may benefit from a more detailed autism evaluation.

Before you begin

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. It takes about 5 minutes to complete. Your responses are sent directly to Dr. Ashish Prakash, who will discuss them with you during your consultation.

Age range: This screening is primarily designed for children between 16 and 30 months of age. Results outside this range may not be reliable. Please continue only if your child is within or near this age range.
What happens next: Fill in the parent and child details, then answer 20 short yes/no questions about your child's everyday behaviour. After you submit, the clinic will be notified and Dr. Ashish will discuss the results with you during your next consultation.
© 2009 Diana Robins, Deborah Fein & Marianne Barton. The M-CHAT-R™ is free to use for clinical, research and educational purposes. The questions are presented in their exact original wording.
