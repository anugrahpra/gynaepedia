01 / The Pediatrician

Children grow up once. Their care should be unhurried, expert, and unwavering.

MBBS · MD Pediatrics · DNB · AIIMS

Dr. Ashish Prakash

Consultation fee: ₹700

A pediatrician and neonatologist with over 30 years of practice, Dr. Ashish Prakash is among the most-respected child specialists in Ghaziabad and the Delhi NCR. He completed his MBBS and MD from AIIMS, New Delhi. Before his Australian fellowship, he served as Senior Resident at Mowasat Hospital, Kuwait (1992–93), then trained in advanced neonatology at the Children's Hospital at Westmead and Royal Alexandra Hospital for Children in Sydney (1993–97).

He set up the first Level III Neonatal Unit in western Uttar Pradesh — bringing ventilation, surfactant therapy, and parenteral nutrition for premature babies to Ghaziabad, so families no longer needed to be referred to Delhi. Today he is Senior Consultant and Head of Neonatology at Yashoda Super Speciality Hospitals, and leads care for newborns, premature babies, and children up to adolescence.

02 / The Gynaecologist

A surgeon's precision. A teacher's patience. A woman's understanding.

MBBS · MD · FRCOG (London)

Dr. Neera Bhan

Consultation fee: ₹800

Dr. Neera Bhan is a senior gynaecologist, obstetrician and laparoscopic surgeon with three decades of practice across Kuwait, Australia, the United Kingdom and India. She completed her MBBS and MD in Obstetrics & Gynaecology from Lady Hardinge Medical College, New Delhi. She received her MRCOG from the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists, London in 2007, and her Fellowship (FRCOG) in 2019.

Her surgical training is exceptional: she holds a Diploma in Advanced Laparoscopic Surgery from France, and trained under Dr. Roger Fay, an Australian pioneer in laparoscopic gynaecology. She served as Senior Resident at Mowasat Hospital, Kuwait (1992–93) and Senior Registrar in Obstetrics & Gynaecology at Nepean Hospital, Sydney (1993–97). Back in India, she was Professor at Santosh Hospital (1997–2005), Senior Consultant at Fortis Hospital, Noida (2007–2017), and Senior Consultant at Columbia Asia / Manipal Hospital, Ghaziabad (2017–2024).

She also runs a popular YouTube channel, Gynae Pedia, dedicated to women's health awareness — explaining periods, PCOS, menopause, fertility and surgery in clear, accessible language for tens of thousands of viewers.

05 / Frequently asked

Questions parents and patients actually ask us.

Who is the best pediatrician in Ghaziabad?
Dr. Ashish Prakash is among the most experienced pediatricians and neonatologists in Ghaziabad — AIIMS-trained, with advanced neonatology training at Westmead Hospital, Sydney, and over 30 years of clinical practice. He set up the first Level III Neonatal Unit in western Uttar Pradesh and is currently Head of Neonatology at Yashoda Super Speciality Hospitals.
I need a gynaecologist for laparoscopic surgery in Ghaziabad — who do you recommend?
Dr. Neera Bhan specialises in laparoscopic and endoscopic gynae surgery, hysteroscopy and minimally-invasive procedures. She is a Fellow of the Royal College of Obstetricians and Gynaecologists, London (FRCOG), trained in laparoscopic surgery in Australia under Dr. Roger Fay and in endoscopic surgery in France.
Do you handle high-risk pregnancies and painless childbirth?
Yes. Dr. Neera Bhan has decades of experience in high-risk obstetrics, painless (epidural) delivery and complex caesarean cases. Newborns are looked after immediately by Dr. Ashish Prakash's neonatology team — making this one of the few practices in Ghaziabad where mother and baby are cared for by senior specialists in continuity.
Do you offer childhood vaccinations and well-baby check-ups?
Absolutely. Dr. Ashish Prakash provides the full IAP (Indian Academy of Pediatrics) recommended immunisation schedule for newborns, infants, children and adolescents — along with growth monitoring, developmental assessment and nutrition advice.
I'm going through menopause / PCOS — can you help?
Yes. Dr. Neera Bhan treats menopause, perimenopause, PCOS, PCOD, abnormal uterine bleeding and hormonal disorders. She also runs a popular YouTube channel on these subjects, Gynae Pedia, where you can preview her approach before booking a consultation.
How do I book an appointment?
Call or WhatsApp 9560517234. The clinic is at R-12/28, Raj Nagar, Ghaziabad and serves patients from across the Delhi NCR — Noida, Indirapuram, Vasundhara, Vaishali, Kaushambi and Meerut. Tele-consultation is available for follow-up cases and patients living away from Ghaziabad.
Do both doctors see patients at the same location?
Yes — Dr. Ashish Prakash and Dr. Neera Bhan run a joint practice. This is especially valuable for families: pregnancy and delivery care from Dr. Bhan, with newborn and pediatric care from Dr. Prakash, all under one roof and one phone number.
